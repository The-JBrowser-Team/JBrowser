Why JBrowser

A calmer way to keep a lot of the web open.

An endless canvas

Cards sit side by side instead of hiding behind tabs. Resize them, split the screen 50/50 or into thirds, and scroll past either end to pull in a new one.

Isolated Spaces

Home, Work, or your own. Every space has its own cookies, storage and sign-ins, so work never leaks into home. Incognito spaces leave nothing behind.

The Gallery

Every card in a space, or in all of them, as a grid of live pictures. Filter, open, close and drag cards between spaces, all from the keyboard if you like.

The Lazy Toolbar

One box for addresses, searches, open cards, bookmarks, history, passwords and more than a hundred commands. Press Ctrl+T or Ctrl+K and type.

Private by default

EasyList and EasyPrivacy block ads and trackers and hide the empty boxes they leave. Phishing and malware protection, secure DNS and link cleaning are on from the start.

Light on memory

Cards you can't see stop drawing after five seconds, and idle ones go to sleep. Calls, music, downloads and unsaved forms are never interrupted.

Made for Windows 11

Acrylic or Mica glass, Snap Layouts, rounded corners and Segoe UI, plus ten gentle colour tints. Incognito spaces turn the whole window black.

Updates itself

New versions come straight from GitHub Releases. JBrowser checks each download's SHA-256 fingerprint before it installs anything.

Keyboard first

One box for everything.

The Lazy Toolbar replaces the new-tab page and the address bar. Type an address, a search, or a keyword like yt, gh or w. It fuzzy-finds your open cards in every space, bookmarks, history and saved passwords, and runs any command: switch DNS, toggle a proxy, split the screen.

Prefixes narrow it down: > commands, @ cards, * bookmarks, # history, $ passwords.

Privacy

Ads and trackers stop at the door.

JBrowser reads the full EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists with its own Adblock Plus engine, so it blocks the same things the big blockers do, and hides the empty ad boxes before the page first appears. It still looks like ordinary Chrome to the sites you visit, so you see fewer "unusual traffic" checks.

  • Phishing and malware sites stopped before they load
  • Secure DNS (Quad9) and optional HTTPS-first
  • Tracking codes removed from links, like utm_source and fbclid
  • An encrypted password manager, locked to your Windows account
