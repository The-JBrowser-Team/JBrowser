Out now · the biggest update yet

JBrowser 2.0 is here.

Stack your cards into columns, read articles without the clutter, and download with more confidence, all in a cleaner, calmer ribbon. Free, as always.

Get JBrowser 2.0 Everything that's new Watch on YouTube

Already have JBrowser? It updates itself: look for Update on the ribbon.

Stack cards

Up to three cards in one column. Drag a card onto another, or press the new stack button. Cards in a column always share one width.

  • Alt+Shift+S
  • Saved with your session

Right where you want it

The stack button opens a space below your card: pick a new page, a card that's already open, a favourite or a bookmark, and set the column's width.

  • Right-click for split views

Reading mode

Just the article's text and pictures: no ads, no menus. Serif or sans, four colour themes and any text size. JBrowser suggests it once while you read.

  • F9
  • Powered by Mozilla Readability

Safer downloads

Programs, files from dangerous sites and files from sites without HTTPS are flagged. Keep them or delete them safely, or let strict mode block them.

A cleaner ribbon

The shield moved into the address bar. Downloads appear only when you download something, with the percentage right there.

Fixes itself

Google sign-in trouble? Press Fix and sign in again. No more colour warp when you minimise or maximise.

Plainer words

Settings and messages rewritten to be shorter and easier to read, for everyone.

  • Alt+Shift+D splits 50/50

  • Drag cards to reorder
