# Code of conduct

JBrowser is made by people who want the web to feel good again. Help keep this project a place where anyone can take
part, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem obvious.
- Keep discussions on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision once it is made.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spam.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every JBrowser space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place you represent the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the **Security → Report a vulnerability** form on
GitHub (it reaches only them), marking the report as a conduct issue. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, warn someone, or block them from the project, depending on what happened.
